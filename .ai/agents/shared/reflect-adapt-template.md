# Reflect & Adapt Document

Assess the friction encountered during the workflow execution, including:
- **Violations & Showstoppers**: Any broken rules or blocking issues encountered.
- **Process Friction / Workflow Gaps**: Steps that were confusing, missing, or inefficient.
- **Tooling Friction / Missing Capabilities**: Tool limitations that caused delays or workarounds.
- **Other Friction**: Anything else that caused delays, confusion, or inefficiencies.

Identify **Root Causes** for any issues encountered.

Identify specific areas where the workflow could be improved, and propose **actionable changes** to address these issues. This promotes continuous learning and improvement.
